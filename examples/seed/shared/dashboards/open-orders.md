# Open orders (example dashboard card)

What it answers: which orders are open, late or waiting on a document. The link is in `url`; the system of record
decides who can see the data. This card never holds figures, exports or screenshots (STANDARDS rule 6a).

## How to read it
- Late column: anything past its promised date. Open the order and add a note before the end of the day.
