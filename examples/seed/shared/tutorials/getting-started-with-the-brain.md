# Getting started with the brain

Connect the brain to your AI assistant, set it up for how you work, and find your role portal (about 15 minutes).

## Before you start
- Your work sign-in (the same account you use for email). Access comes from that account; there is nothing separate
  to remember.

## Steps
1. Follow [[how-to/connect-the-brain-to-claude-and-chatgpt|Connect the brain to Claude or ChatGPT]] and sign in
   with your work account.
2. Ask your assistant: "How do you want your brain to work for you?" It runs the
   [[how-to/brain-onboarding-interview|onboarding interview]] and saves your preferences to your own private area
   only after you confirm them.
3. Ask your assistant to open the staff map in the shared area (`reference/start-here` in the `shared` silo) and pick
   your role under "For staff". Your portal is `roles/<your role>` in the same silo.
4. Ask a question about your work. The assistant looks in your own area and the shared area first, cites the pages it
   used, and says when the brain does not know.
5. Try a date: "What was I working on last Tuesday?" The assistant answers from your own area's timeline.
6. At the end of a real working session, say "save this session". The assistant records a session page plus any
   tasks, notes and decisions in your own area.
