---
description: Health-check a silo of the brain
---

Run the lint on a local copy of the silo (`python -m tools.wiki pull ...` then `python -m tools.wiki lint --wiki <folder>`), or read `reference/start-here` and `reference/tasks` through the connector. Fix broken links and missing frontmatter with `wiki_write`. For orphans, link them from the right project or concept page. Then use your own judgment on what a script cannot see: contradictions between pages, claims a newer page supersedes, concepts mentioned but lacking a page, employee pages past their `review_by` date. Report findings briefly and propose fixes before making large changes. Never edit `index.md` or `log.md` by hand; every write maintains them.
